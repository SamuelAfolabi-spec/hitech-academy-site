Problem · Knowledge Leak

Every year, we produce top‑tier graduates who disappear into global industries. We lose their data, their stories, and their potential to give back.

Problem · Untapped Capital

No formal, school‑owned Connect site

Our alumni are in Tech, Oil & Gas, Law, and Medicine across the continent. Currently, there is no formal, school‑owned "Connect" site to facilitate a mentorship culture.

What We're Building

Two platforms, one community.

The Student Repository — Blog

A professional portfolio where students document research, projects, and creative problem‑solving, serving as a living digital resume.

  • Publishing tools for research & creative work
  • A culture of healthy peer‑to‑peer excellence
  • Portfolio‑ready for international admissions

The Alumni Portal — Connect

A secure, database‑driven hub for Old Students to register, network, mentor, and contribute back to the school community.

  • Verified alumni registration & directory
  • Industry‑aligned mentorship matching
  • Masterclasses & special‑interest groups
The Value Proposition

Built for alumni, students, and the school alike.

Industry‑Aligned Mentorship

The Connect site allows alumni to influence the school's culture of excellence by providing real‑world feedback on student work — ensuring students are market‑ready before they even graduate.

Curriculum Synergy

Alumni in specialized fields — Fintech, Medicine, Creative Arts — can host masterclasses or contribute to special‑interest groups, closing the gap between the Nigerian classroom and global industry.

Peer‑to‑Peer Excellence

The blog creates a culture of healthy competition. When students see their peers publishing high‑level work, it raises the standard of academic ambition for the entire school.

The "Invisible" Internship

By collaborating with alumni on the Connect side, students gain early exposure to corporate standards, professional ethics, and industry language before they even sit for their WASSCE.

The Digital Imprint

Students transition from digital consumers to digital architects — mastering the data management, SEO, and cybersecurity skills required to lead in the global remote economy.

Academic Branding

Prospective parents don't just see a brochure — they see evidence of current students solving problems, proving the school's curriculum is working in real time.
